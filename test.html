Solar System Application

The solar system is a collection of celestial bodies centered around the Sun, which holds the system together with its powerful gravity. Overview of the Solar SystemThe Center: The Sun is a massive star that provides the heat and light needed for life on Earth. The Planets: Eight planets travel around the Sun in fixed paths called orbits. Inner Planets: Mercury, Venus, Earth, and Mars are small and rocky. Outer Planets: Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune are massive gas and ice giants. Other Objects: The system also includes hundreds of moons, dwarf planets like Pluto, and thousands of asteroids and comets. Our Home: Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only place known to support life.
